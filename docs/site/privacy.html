Privacy Policy

Effective date: [DATE] · Contact: [CONTACT EMAIL]

Bookmark Sync is a browser extension that keeps your bookmarks in sync across your own Chromium-based browsers (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Vivaldi). It is a private, non-commercial project maintained by [YOUR NAME] for personal and family use. This policy explains what data the extension handles and where it goes.

Summary

  • Your bookmarks are encrypted on your computer before they are stored in your own Google Drive.
  • The developer runs no servers and receives no data from you — no bookmarks, no account details, no analytics.
  • The extension can only access a hidden app-specific folder in your Google Drive, not your other files.

Google user data

The extension requests a single Google permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata ("See, create, and delete its own configuration data in your Google Drive"). It uses this permission only to store, read, update and delete its own encrypted sync files in the hidden application data folder of the signed-in user's Google Drive. It cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive.

Before anything is uploaded, it is encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM using a key derived from a passphrase that only you know. Google and the developer cannot read the contents. The passphrase is never stored or transmitted.

Bookmark Sync's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically, Google user data is:

  • used only to provide the bookmark-sync feature you see in the extension;
  • not transferred to anyone, except as necessary to provide that feature (to Google Drive itself), to comply with law, or with your explicit consent;
  • not used for advertising, profiling, credit decisions or any other purpose;
  • not read by any human.

Data sharing

No data is sold, shared or sent to the developer or to third parties. The only network services contacted are Google's sign-in and Google Drive APIs (accounts.google.com, oauth2.googleapis.com, www.googleapis.com). The extension contains no analytics, tracking, advertising or remote code.

Data retention and deletion

  • Encrypted sync files remain in your Google Drive until you delete them. Daily encrypted snapshots are kept for up to one year and then deleted automatically.
  • Delete everything at any time from the extension (Options → Security & data → Remove all synced data), or in Google Drive under Settings → Manage apps → Bookmark Sync → Delete hidden app data.
  • Revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling the extension removes its local data from that browser. Your bookmarks themselves are never deleted by uninstalling.

Security

Data is encrypted end-to-end (AES-256-GCM, PBKDF2-SHA256 key derivation with 600,000 iterations); the encryption key is kept on your device as a non-exportable key. All network traffic uses HTTPS. The extension's activity log records counts only, never bookmark titles or URLs.

Children

The extension is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
