Bookmark Sync

A private browser extension that keeps your bookmarks in sync across your Chromium-based browsers — Chrome, Edge, Brave and Vivaldi — on Windows, macOS and Linux.

What it does

  • Add, rename, move or delete a bookmark on one computer and it appears the same way on your others.
  • Works across different browsers, not just one vendor's account sync.
  • Combines bookmarks safely when several computers change things at the same time, and asks before applying large deletions.
  • Keeps daily snapshots you can restore, and lets you export bookmarks as a standard file any browser can import.

How your data is handled

Bookmarks are encrypted on your computer with a passphrase only you know, then stored in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive. The extension asks Google only for access to that app folder (drive.appdata) — it cannot see your other Drive files. There are no developer servers, no analytics and no tracking.

Why it asks for Google sign-in

Google Drive is used purely as encrypted storage so your computers can exchange bookmark changes. Sign-in grants the extension permission to manage its own hidden data folder in your Drive; you can revoke this at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Availability

Bookmark Sync is a personal, non-commercial project used by its developer and family. It is not distributed through any extension store.
